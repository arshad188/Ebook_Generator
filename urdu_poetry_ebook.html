Dil-e-Nadaan
Mirza Ghalib Ghazal
👤 About the Poet: Mirza Asadullah Khan Ghalib (1797-1869) was a renowned Urdu and Persian poet from the Mughal Empire. He is considered one of the most influential poets of the Urdu language.
📝 Summary: This ghazal explores the confusion and pain of love, questioning the nature of the heart's affliction and the possibility of finding a cure.
دلِ ناداں تجھے ہوا کیا ہے
→ O naive heart, what has befallen you?
آخر اس درد کی دوا کیا ہے
→ What is the cure for this pain at last?
ہم کو اُن سے وفا کی امید ہے
→ I hope for loyalty from them,
جانے اُن کو تو بے وفا کیا ہے
→ Who knows what disloyalty means to them?
Khudi Ko Kar Buland Itna
Allama Iqbal
👤 About the Poet: Sir Muhammad Iqbal (1877-1938) was a philosopher, poet, and politician in British India. He is widely regarded as the spiritual father of Pakistan and one of the most important figures in Urdu literature.
📝 Summary: This powerful poem encourages self-elevation and spiritual growth, suggesting that when one reaches a high state of consciousness, even divine will responds to human aspiration.
خودی کو کر بلند اتنا کہ ہر تقدیر سے پہلے
→ Elevate your selfhood so high, that before every fate,
خدا بندے سے خود پوچھے بتا تیری رضا کیا ہے
→ God Himself asks His servant: 'Tell me, what is your will?'
افلاک سے آگے جا کر دیکھا
→ I went beyond the heavens and saw,
خوابوں کی دنیا میں کھو کر دیکھا
→ I lost myself in the world of dreams and saw.
Mujh Se Pehli Si Mohabbat
Faiz Ahmed Faiz Ghazal
👤 About the Poet: Faiz Ahmed Faiz (1911-1984) was a Pakistani poet, writer, and one of the most famous Urdu poets of the 20th century. His poetry is known for its revolutionary themes and romanticism.
📝 Summary: A poignant reflection on how love and idealism change over time, this poem marks the transition from youthful romance to a more mature and politicized understanding of love.
مجھ سے پہلی سی محبت مرے محبوب نہ مانگ
→ Don't ask me for the love I once had, my beloved,
میں نے سمجھا تھا کہ تو ہے تو درخشاں ہے حیات
→ I thought that if you exist, then life is radiant,
تیرا غم ہے تو غمِ دہر کا جھگڑا کیا ہے
→ If your sorrow exists, what trouble is the world's sorrow?
اب وہ نرم گلوں کی باتیں اب وہ شیریں باتیں
→ Now those soft conversations, now those sweet words,
اب وہ افکار کی لذت اب وہ تغیر کی رات
→ Now the pleasure of thoughts, now the night of change.
